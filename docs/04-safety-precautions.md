# 4. Safety Precautions

## 3. Safety Measures

### 3.1 General Safety Guidelines

When operating the MK-56 "Elektronika" microcalculator, observe the following safety precautions to ensure safe operation and prevent damage to the device.

### 3.2 Electrical Safety

#### Power Supply

1. **Voltage Requirements**:
   - Use only the specified power supply voltage
   - Verify voltage matches calculator specifications
   - Do not exceed rated voltage

2. **Power Adapter** (if applicable):
   - Use only approved power adapters
   - Check adapter polarity before connection
   - Inspect adapter for damage before use

3. **Battery Safety**:
   - Install batteries with correct polarity
   - Do not mix old and new batteries
   - Do not mix different battery types
   - Remove batteries if not using for extended period
   - Dispose of batteries properly according to local regulations

4. **Electrical Hazards**:
   - Do not open the calculator case while powered
   - Do not expose internal components to moisture
   - Disconnect power before any maintenance
   - Do not attempt repairs unless qualified

### 3.3 Environmental Safety

#### Operating Environment

1. **Temperature**:
   - Operate within specified temperature range (typically 10°C to 40°C)
   - Avoid extreme heat or cold
   - Do not place near heat sources (radiators, stoves, etc.)
   - Do not leave in direct sunlight for extended periods

2. **Humidity**:
   - Operate in dry conditions (20-80% relative humidity)
   - Avoid condensation
   - Do not expose to rain or water
   - Keep away from humid environments (bathrooms, etc.)

3. **Physical Environment**:
   - Place on stable, flat surface
   - Avoid areas with strong vibrations
   - Keep away from magnetic fields
   - Protect from dust and debris

### 3.4 Handling Precautions

#### Physical Handling

1. **Gentle Operation**:
   - Press keys gently - do not use excessive force
   - Do not drop or subject to impact
   - Handle with clean, dry hands
   - Avoid placing heavy objects on calculator

2. **Transport and Storage**:
   - Use protective case when transporting
   - Store in dry, cool place
   - Avoid stacking heavy items on top
   - Keep away from children when not in use

3. **Cleaning**:
   - Power off before cleaning
   - Use soft, dry cloth for exterior
   - Do not use solvents or harsh chemicals
   - Do not spray liquids directly on calculator
   - Clean keyboard with compressed air if needed

### 3.5 Operational Safety

#### Proper Use

1. **Intended Use**:
   - Use only for calculation purposes
   - Do not modify or alter the calculator
   - Do not use damaged calculator
   - Follow all operating instructions

2. **Continuous Operation**:
   - The calculator can operate continuously
   - Monitor for overheating during extended use
   - Allow cooling periods if device becomes warm
   - Power off when not in use to conserve battery

3. **Display Care**:
   - Do not press on display
   - Avoid scratching display surface
   - Do not expose display to extreme temperatures
   - Protect from direct sunlight when not in use

### 3.6 Maintenance Safety

#### Service and Repair

1. **Qualified Service**:
   - Only qualified technicians should perform internal repairs
   - Do not attempt to open sealed components
   - Use only specified replacement parts
   - Follow proper ESD (Electrostatic Discharge) precautions

2. **User Maintenance**:
   - Limit user maintenance to battery replacement and cleaning
   - Power off before any maintenance
   - Disconnect external power before opening battery compartment
   - Verify correct battery polarity during replacement

### 3.7 Battery Safety

#### Battery Handling

1. **Installation**:
   - Observe correct polarity markings
   - Ensure batteries are fully seated
   - Close battery compartment securely
   - Verify operation after installation

2. **Rechargeable Batteries** (if applicable):
   - Use only specified charger
   - Do not overcharge
   - Monitor charging process
   - Disconnect when fully charged
   - Do not charge damaged batteries

3. **Battery Disposal**:
   - Do not dispose in regular trash
   - Follow local recycling regulations
   - Do not incinerate batteries
   - Keep away from children

### 3.8 Emergency Procedures

#### Malfunction Response

1. **Display Issues**:
   - Power off immediately
   - Check power supply
   - Allow to cool if warm
   - Consult service technician if problem persists

2. **Smoke or Odor**:
   - Power off immediately
   - Disconnect power source
   - Remove batteries if safe to do so
   - Do not use until inspected by qualified technician
   - Ventilate area

3. **Physical Damage**:
   - Power off immediately
   - Do not use damaged calculator
   - Inspect for battery leakage
   - Consult service technician for repair assessment

4. **Liquid Exposure**:
   - Power off immediately
   - Remove batteries
   - Do not power on
   - Allow to dry completely (several days)
   - Have inspected by technician before use

### 3.9 Data Safety

#### Protecting Calculations and Programs

1. **Memory Preservation**:
   - Some models retain memory when powered off
   - Use fresh batteries to prevent data loss
   - Back up important programs by recording on paper
   - Test programs after power interruption

2. **Critical Calculations**:
   - Verify important calculations manually or with second calculator
   - Use appropriate precision for application
   - Understand limitations of calculator accuracy
   - Document calculation procedures

### 3.10 Regulatory Compliance

#### Standards and Certifications

1. **Electrical Safety**:
   - Complies with applicable Soviet/Russian electrical standards
   - Modern equivalents should meet current safety standards
   - Verify certifications for your region

2. **Electromagnetic Compatibility**:
   - May be sensitive to strong electromagnetic fields
   - Keep away from radio transmitters
   - Avoid use near powerful magnets
   - May interfere with sensitive electronic equipment

### 3.11 Child Safety

#### Supervision Required

1. **Not a Toy**:
   - Keep away from young children
   - Supervise children during use
   - Teach proper handling procedures
   - Store securely when not in use

2. **Battery Safety**:
   - Batteries are choking hazard
   - Keep batteries away from children
   - Supervise battery replacement
   - Ensure battery compartment is secure

### 3.12 Long-Term Storage

#### Extended Non-Use

1. **Preparation for Storage**:
   - Power off calculator
   - Remove batteries to prevent leakage
   - Clean exterior
   - Store in protective case
   - Keep in dry, cool environment

2. **Periodic Checks**:
   - Inspect stored calculator periodically
   - Check for corrosion or damage
   - Test operation before returning to service
   - Replace batteries as needed

## Warning Symbols and Indicators

### Common Warning Indicators

- **⚠ Warning**: Indicates potential hazard
- **⚡ Electrical Hazard**: Risk of electric shock
- **🔥 Fire Hazard**: Risk of fire or heat damage
- **☠ Toxic**: Battery chemicals hazardous if exposed
- **♻ Recycling**: Proper disposal required

In case of serious malfunction or safety concern:
1. Discontinue use immediately
2. Contact qualified service technician
