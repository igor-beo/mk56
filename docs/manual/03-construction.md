# Construction

Translated from «Микрокалькулятор Электроника МК56. Техническое описание и инструкция по эксплуатации». Original scan: [references/MK-56 inst.pdf](../../references/MK-56%20inst.pdf).

*Transcription conventions: the calculator uses a decimal comma, and displayed values are given exactly as printed (e.g. `12,56637`). The typescript prints the digit 1 as «I»; it is given here as 1. The original prints operation codes in seven-segment glyphs (`-` = A, `L` = B, `С` = C, `Г` = D, `Е` = E, blank = F); they are given here in ordinary hexadecimal, e.g. `5D`. The ÷ symbol in the original closely resembles +; the two keys are identified by their codes (13 = ÷, 10 = +).*

## 2.3. Construction

*(p. 19)*

2.3.1. All elements of the electrical circuit, except the mains cord ending in a plug and the mains switch, are placed on two printed circuit boards made of glass-fibre laminate.

2.3.2. The input unit board carries the Г/Р/ГРД (degree-radian-grad) switch, whose slider is brought out to the front panel of the calculator, and three keyboard blocks with thirty keys.

2.3.3. The electronic unit board is the main board of the calculator. It carries all the LSIs of the calculator, the display control microcircuits, the twelve-digit display, and all elements of the voltage converter except the mains switch, which is fixed to the cover of the calculator.

2.3.4. Connections between the boards and the mains switch are made with flexible wires. The mains cord is soldered to the mains switch and brought out through the rear opening in the cover.

2.3.5. The calculator case consists of a cover and a base made of impact-resistant plastic. The front panel is laid on the cover, and the input unit board is fixed to it with screws. The operational unit board is fixed to bosses on the base. The base is joined to the cover and the panel by 4 screws, which are recessed in sockets used for applying a seal.
