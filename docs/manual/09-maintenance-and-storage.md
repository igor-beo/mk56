# 8–9. Maintenance; transport and storage

Translated from «Микрокалькулятор Электроника МК56. Техническое описание и инструкция по эксплуатации». Original scan: [references/MK-56 inst.pdf](../../references/MK-56%20inst.pdf).

*Transcription conventions: the calculator uses a decimal comma, and displayed values are given exactly as printed (e.g. `12,56637`). The typescript prints the digit 1 as «I»; it is given here as 1. The original prints operation codes in seven-segment glyphs (`-` = A, `L` = B, `С` = C, `Г` = D, `Е` = E, blank = F); they are given here in ordinary hexadecimal, e.g. `5D`. The ÷ symbol in the original closely resembles +; the two keys are identified by their codes (13 = ÷, 10 = +).*

## 8. MAINTENANCE OF THE CALCULATOR

*(p. 94)*

8.1. Maintenance of the calculator must be carried out by a specialist who has studied this technical description and operating instructions.

8.2. The correct operation of the calculator should be checked using the calculator functional test given in the appendix.

## 9. TRANSPORT AND STORAGE

*(p. 95)*

9.1. Calculators may be transported in transport packaging by any type of transport over any distance under conditions not exceeding the following:

- ambient temperature from minus 50 to +50 °C;
- relative air humidity 95% at a temperature of 30 °C;
- atmospheric pressure from 86 to 106 kPa (645–795 mm Hg), shock loads with an acceleration of 147 m/s² (15g) and a pulse duration of 5–10 ms.

The packaging in which the calculators are transported must be protected from direct exposure to atmospheric precipitation, solar radiation and mechanical damage.

9.2. The calculator must be stored in a dry heated room, in the manufacturer's packaging, at an air temperature from 1 to 40 °C, with a relative air humidity of no more than 90% and in the absence of acid, alkaline and other aggressive impurities in the air.

Storage period: no more than 1 year.
