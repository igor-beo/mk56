# Safety Instructions and Preparation for Operation

Translated from «Микрокалькулятор Электроника МК56. Техническое описание и инструкция по эксплуатации». Original scan: [references/MK-56 inst.pdf](../../references/MK-56%20inst.pdf).

*Transcription conventions: the calculator uses a decimal comma, and displayed values are given exactly as printed (e.g. `12,56637`). The typescript prints the digit 1 as «I»; it is given here as 1. The original prints operation codes in seven-segment glyphs (`-` = A, `L` = B, `С` = C, `Г` = D, `Е` = E, blank = F); they are given here in ordinary hexadecimal, e.g. `5D`. The ÷ symbol in the original closely resembles +; the two keys are identified by their codes (13 = ÷, 10 = +).*

---

## 3. SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

*(p. 20)*

3.1. Persons operating the calculator and repair specialists must receive safety instruction for work with electronic equipment.

3.2. The calculator contains elements at a voltage of 220 V and at a direct voltage of minus 35 V; it is therefore strictly forbidden to dismantle or repair the calculator while it is switched on.

---

## 4. PREPARING THE CALCULATOR FOR OPERATION

*(p. 20)*

4.1. Switch on the calculator by setting the power switch to the position marked with a dot. The image "С" should appear in the most significant digit position of the display, indicating that the calculator is ready for operation.
